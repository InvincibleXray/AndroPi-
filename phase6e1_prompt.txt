============================================================

- whether model is one-to-many or one-to-one
- maximum detections

IMPORTANT:

YOLO26 supports different detection export paths.

The implementation must determine the ACTUAL output contract
of the exact artifact being integrated.

For YOLO26 detection:

    NMS/raw one-to-many export:
        output may follow a raw prediction layout and require
        external confidence filtering + NMS.

    NMS-free one-to-one export:
        output may follow a processed detection layout such as:

        [x1, y1, x2, y2, confidence, class_id]

        with a bounded number of detections.

DO NOT hardcode either format unless the actual model artifact
proves that format.

DO NOT blindly reuse YOLO11n's:

    640x640
    [1,84,8400]
    80-class assumptions
    decoder
    DFL assumptions

YOLO26 uses a different detection head design and can be
exported with different output behavior.

The detector must inspect/validate the model contract at
initialization.

If the tensor contract is unsupported:

    fail honestly
    activeModel = unavailable
    provide exact reason

Do NOT silently reinterpret incompatible tensors.
